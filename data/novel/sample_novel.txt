Chapter 1: The Visitor

Inspector Rao arrived at the old mansion just as the storm broke. The
housekeeper, Mrs. Iyer, met him at the door with a lantern trembling in
her hand. "It's the master's ledger," she said. "It has vanished from
the study, and with it, I fear, the truth about his fortune."

Rao stepped into the hallway, noting the muddy footprints leading away
from the study door. Someone had left in a hurry, and recently.

Chapter 2: The Study

The study smelled of old paper and spilled ink. A single drawer of the
mahogany desk hung open, empty except for a torn corner of a page.
Rao held it to the candlelight: a fragment of handwriting, half a
number, and the edge of a wax seal.

"Who else knew the ledger was kept here?" Rao asked.

Mrs. Iyer hesitated. "Only the master's nephew, and perhaps the
accountant who visited last Tuesday."
